# Figma Handoff

Optional. Figma holds an approved review copy and archive. Paper stays the editable master. Writing to Figma files outside your drafts needs a Full seat.

- One page per flow (e.g. "Welcome Flow - LIVE"), plus one page of reusable assets
- Each email gets a Desktop frame at 600 px and a Phone frame at 375 px, side by side on the open canvas. No boxes, borders or background panels around them
- Name frames with the email, device, exact size and ratio: "Welcome 1 / Desktop / 600 x 2550px / 4:17" and "Welcome 1 / Mobile / 375 x 1593.75px / 4:17". Keep the ratio exact, never round the artwork to fit a name
- Above each pair, as editable text: email name, subject, preview text, timing and main link, who gets it, notes
- Import the approved 2x export. Never rebuild the design in Figma
- Split tall images into stacked tiles no taller than 4,096 px, with zero gap, no strokes and no effects. Check the tile heights add up
- The phone frame is the same image scaled down, not a separate phone layout. Say so in the notes
- Before reporting it done, look at the actual canvas at both sizes. Long imports have reported success and then shown blank
- Add only genuinely reusable parts to the assets page, with transparent padding kept around badges and shadows
