# QA Checklist

Run all of it before an email goes live. Automated checks prove files and links, not taste or delivery.

## Design (a person checks)

- [ ] The full scroll reads as one designed environment, not stacked blocks
- [ ] The hero, middle and join were approved together
- [ ] Logo is the official file. Product matches the real thing in every image
- [ ] Text is readable at 375 and 320 px, including the smallest line
- [ ] Buttons are big and bold. No small labels above headlines
- [ ] Badges, icons and panels are aligned and nothing is clipped
- [ ] The footer starts with a clean hard cut, with the right content for this audience

## Copy and offer

- [ ] Subject, preview text, artwork copy and links all match the approved version
- [ ] Offers and claims checked against the live store and the claims page today
- [ ] The neutral version has no offer, gifts or benefits meant for qualifying carts
- [ ] No currency amounts in flow emails if the store sells in more than one currency

## Files and code

- [ ] Every strip is under 2,000 px tall at 2x and under 1 MB
- [ ] Strips reassemble exactly (the cut-email tool reports this)
- [ ] Colours converted to sRGB, transparent areas filled
- [ ] Every strip has its link and real alt text. Footer buttons each have their own link
- [ ] Zero padding between strips in the platform
- [ ] Unsubscribe, preferences and address are live text and work

## In the platform

- [ ] Preview with a real person's cart or a recent real event, not sample data
- [ ] Recovery links land on that person's cart or checkout, in their currency
- [ ] One test with a qualifying cart, one without
- [ ] Test sends only to recipients named for this run
- [ ] Checked in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, on a real phone
- [ ] Checked with images off and in dark mode
- [ ] Scrolled slowly on a phone looking for lines between strips

## Going live

- [ ] Explicit go-ahead for this exact email and destination
- [ ] Backup saved of whatever is being replaced
- [ ] Only the content changed. Triggers, delays, exits, splits and people in the flow untouched
- [ ] Read back after the change: what's live matches what was approved
- [ ] Build record updated
